LishePoa product validation

How accurately do three AI models (GPT-6-Sol, Gemini 3.8 Flash, Claude Opus 5.5) read food product labels, compared with the human-typed ground truth? Three ways of checking, side by side.

① Regex comparison

Rule-based, fully automatic. Text and numbers are normalized with regular expressions, every field gets a 0–100% score, and a product passes when its weighted score is at least 80%.

Open regex comparison →

② LLM judge

GPT-4o mini grades text fields blind (models shown as A/B/C); numbers and codes are checked exactly in Python. Marks are ✓ / ✗ / ~, and the target is 85%.

Open LLM judge →

③ Manual review

Clive, Abigail and Roda each check batches of 33 random products by hand, ticking ✓/✗ per model in six categories. This is the human reference that keeps both automated methods honest.

Open manual review →

The six comparison categories

CategoryWhat it covers
IdentityProduct name, brand, manufacturer, category, country of origin, barcode
PackagePackage size and unit, count, package type
Nutrition basisPer 100 g / per 100 ml / per serving, serving size
NutrientsEvery nutrient value with its unit and qualifier (<, min, max, range)
IngredientsThe ingredient list as printed, including E-numbers and percentages
Other label informationStorage, allergen, usage and other printed text

Running it

Start the site with python3 serve.py and open http://localhost:8000. Use python3 serve.py --lan to let teammates on the same network review on your server. Rebuild the regex results with python3 compare.py and the LLM-judge results with .venv/bin/python judge_eval.py. Saved judge answers mean reruns cost nothing.
