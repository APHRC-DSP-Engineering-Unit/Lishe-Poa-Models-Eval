How the checking works
  • Each model read photos of the same product labels. People typed the same labels by hand; that is the ground truth.
  • Products are matched across files by their product ID. Products not in every file (such as F406) are left out.
  • Text fields (name, brand, manufacturer, country, package type, ingredients, other label info) are graded by the judge model, which sees the models only as A, B and C.
  • Numbers and codes (nutrients, barcode, sizes, category, nutrition basis) must match exactly; 1.5 l equals 1500 ml.
  • A partly correct answer for ingredients or other label info counts as not correct. Fields empty in the ground truth and in every model are not counted.
  • When all models disagree with the ground truth on the same value, the ground truth or the unit may be the problem.
